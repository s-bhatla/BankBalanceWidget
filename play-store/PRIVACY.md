# Bank Balance privacy policy

**Draft for publication. Add the developer’s privacy contact before hosting.**

Bank Balance is an Android app that uses transaction SMS from supported banks to show account balances and spending on home-screen widgets.

## SMS access

If you grant inbox access, Bank Balance checks up to 100 recent inbox messages when you start an account scan or tap refresh. If you grant incoming SMS access, the app checks new SMS in the background so matching bank alerts can update your balance and spending widgets. Message text is processed on your device. Full SMS bodies are not saved by the app.

## Information kept on your device

Bank Balance stores the bank sender identifier, visible account ending, balances, transaction amounts and timestamps, transaction deduplication identifiers, widget settings, and the last manual refresh time. This information is used to calculate balances and spending, avoid duplicate transactions, and show the widgets you configure.

The app has no account sign-in, advertising, analytics, internet permission, or cloud sync. It does not send app data or SMS to the developer or to third parties. If you open the public privacy policy, your browser loads that website separately.

## Your choices

You can decline or revoke SMS access in Android settings. Revoking access stops the corresponding scan or update feature. To remove information that Bank Balance has saved, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Removing a home-screen widget removes its display.
